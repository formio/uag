What to notice

Speed where it is safe. People where it is not.

Pages chosen by triage

Steps appear in the rail as your description is read, each saying why. They are ordinary Form.io conditionals on the fields the decision engine answered.

Confidence, not just answers

When triage is unsure of the claim type, a "Check our understanding" step appears. Weak fraud signals are flagged for a person, never cleared by the browser.

Your input wins

Change a suggested value, then edit the description: the plugin reads it again and refills only what you have not touched.

Build it yourself

One call to attach.

The renderer plugin connects any formio.js form or wizard to /agent/extract. It streams decisions as they arrive, waits briefly on Next so conditional pages are in place, and asks once more for the pages triage added.

  • Give it the signed-in user's own UAG token, so extraction runs with their permissions.
  • Mark fields with uag: triage; tune with uagThreshold and uagUncertainBand.
  • Measure thresholds on your own data with uag-eval before you rely on them.

  formName: 'claim',
  task: 'triage',
  trigger: 'next',                 // read the description on Continue
  getToken: () => session.uagToken, // the user's own token
  onStart: () => showReading(true),
  onDone: (summary) => showReading(false),
});
